---
description: Keeps Workbench facts, decisions, implementation steps, and explorations in their designated project documents.
alwaysApply: true
---

Use `docs/plans/<project>/` for Workbench artifacts.

- `research.md`: facts, existing behavior, external evidence, capabilities, and options. No decisions or implementation steps.
- `design.md`: approved decisions, rationale, trade-offs, scope, and boundaries. Do not repeat research without connecting it to a decision; no implementation checklist.
- `tasks.md`: executable implementation phases, file changes, tests, dependencies, and checkpoints. No new rationale or unverified research.
- `thoughts/`: questions, experiments, alternatives, and refinements that are not ready for the formal documents.

Classify each addition before writing: fact or option to research; decision or rationale to design; implementation step to tasks; uncertainty or exploration to thoughts.
